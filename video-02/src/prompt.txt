Meri dukaan ke liye ek single-file HTML tool banao, naam "Udhaar Khata".

• Form: customer ka naam, 10 ank ka mobile, rakam,
  aur "Udhaar diya" ya "Paisa aaya".
• Ek hi mobile ki saari entry ek khate mein judein.
• List: sabse zyada baaki upar. Har row pe "₹X baaki" (laal)
  ya "Hisaab saaf" (hara), aur aakhri entry kitne din pehle.
• Upar 4 ginti: kul baaki, kitne log, aaj udhaar diya,
  aaj paisa aaya.
• "WhatsApp yaad dilao" button: wa.me link, naam aur baaki
  rakam ke saath pehle se likha vinamra message.
• "Paisa aaya" button: form mein naam bhar de, bas rakam likhni ho.
• Data sirf browser ke localStorage mein rahe. CSV download do.
• Koi server, login ya paid service nahi. Mobile pe bhi chale.
