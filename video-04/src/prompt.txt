Meri coaching ke liye ek single-file HTML tool banao, naam "Fees Register".

• Form: student ka naam, parent ka 10 ank ka mobile, batch,
  mahine ki fees, aur kis mahine se aa raha hai.
• Us mahine se aaj tak, jis mahine ki fees jama nahi hui,
  wo apne aap "baaki" mein dikhe. Naya mahina = nayi fees baaki.
• List: sabse zyada baaki upar. Har row pe baaki mahine aur
  rakam (laal), ya "jama" (hara).
• Upar 4 ginti: kul fees baaki, kitne students par baaki,
  is mahine kitni jama hui, kul students.
• "Fees aayi" button: sabse purana baaki mahina jama kare.
  Galti ho to wapas lene ka button.
• "WhatsApp yaad dilao" button: wa.me link, student ka naam,
  mahine aur rakam ke saath pehle se likha vinamra message.
• Data sirf browser ke localStorage mein rahe. CSV download do.
• Koi server, login ya paid service nahi. Mobile pe bhi chale.
