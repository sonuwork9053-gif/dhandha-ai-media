## English narration

**1. Hook** `[ON SCREEN: same as Hindi]`
Your customer's filter was due ten days ago and nobody called. Why? You never knew. And that is how you lose them.

**2. The turn**
In this video I am showing a free tool that AI built. Every morning it tells you which customer to remind today. And at the end you get the full prompt, so you can have your own tool made.

**3. The problem**
In most small businesses, follow-up lives in a diary, or in someone's memory. By the time you remember, the customer has gone somewhere else. Up here are four counts: how many are overdue, how many are due today, how many are coming in the next seven days, and your total customers.

**4. Adding a customer**
Adding a customer takes one line. Name, mobile number, the job, the last date, and how many days until the next reminder. Press add, and they land in the right place in the list.

**5. The list**
The list always sorts by urgency. The most overdue customer sits at the top, in red. Today's are in orange. The ones with time left are in green, lower down.

**6. WhatsApp**
Now the real work. Press the WhatsApp button. A message opens, already written, with the customer's name and the job. You only press send. And the tool remembers the day the reminder went out.

**7. Job done**
Service finished? Press job done. The next date moves forward by itself, and the customer drops down the list. In ninety days they come back to the top on their own.

**8. Your data**
All of it stays on your own phone or computer, not on any server. And you can download the whole list for Excel whenever you want.

**9. The payoff**
Here is the prompt I promised. Paste it into any AI chat, write in your own kind of work, and your own follow-up tool is ready. The full prompt is in the description.

**10. The close**
Which job should AI do next? Write it in the comments. The next video will be on that.

```
- "Kaam ho gaya" button: pichhli tareekh aaj kar do, agli due date apne aap badhe.
- Data sirf browser ke localStorage mein rahe. CSV download ka button do.
- Koi server, login ya paid service nahi. Mobile pe bhi theek chale.
```
